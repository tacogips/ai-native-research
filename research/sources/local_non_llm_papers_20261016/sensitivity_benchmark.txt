C. Leakage Removal Protocol
   One of the most important challenges during the preprocess-
ing of WikiLeaks PlusD cables is the removal of remaining
classification artefacts that are embedded in document bodies.

shortcuts instead of learning real sensitivity signals. Three
types of leakage were found and eliminated:
