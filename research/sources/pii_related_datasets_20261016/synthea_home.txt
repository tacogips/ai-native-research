                    <div class="text-card eq-h-md">
                        <h2>Generic Module Framework</h2>
                        <h3>Modeling the Progression and Treatment of Disease</h3>
                        <p>
                            Synthea’s Generic Module Framework (GMF) enables the modeling of various diseases and conditions that contribute to the medical history of synthetic patients. Each patient is simulated independently from birth to present day. Their diseases, conditions and medical care are defined by one or more generic modules.
                        </p>
                        <p>
                            Each module models events that could occur in a real patient’s life,  describing a progression of states and the transitions between them. These modules are informed by clinicians and real-world statistics collected by the CDC, NIH, and other research sources. Synthea started with modules for  the top ten reasons patients visit their primary care physician and the top ten conditions that result in years of life lost. Check out our <a href="https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea/tree/gh-pages/graphviz" onclick="trackOutboundLink('https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea/tree/gh-pages/graphviz'); return false;">full gallery of modules</a> to see what we've added since.
                        </p>

                        <h2>Patient Data</h2>
                        <h3>Modern Healthcare and Research Data Standards</h3>
                        <p>
                            For each synthetic patient, Synthea data contains a complete medical history, including medications, allergies, medical encounters, and social determinants of health. This data can be used without concern for legal or privacy restrictions. To support developers, clinicians and researchers alike, Synthea data is exported in a variety of data standards, including <a href="http://hl7.org/fhir/" onclick="trackOutboundLink('http://hl7.org/fhir/'); return false;">HL7 FHIR®</a>, <a href="https://www.healthit.gov/policy-researchers-implementers/consolidated-cda-overview" onclick="trackOutboundLink('https://www.healthit.gov/policy-researchers-implementers/consolidated-cda-overview'); return false;">C-CDA</a> and CSV.
                        </p>
                        <p>
                            Use the buttons <span class="hidden-sm hidden-xs">to the left</span><span class="hidden-xl hidden-lg hidden-md">below</span> to download over a thousand sample patients in the available formats. You can also <a href="https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea/wiki/Basic-Setup-and-Running" onclick="trackOutboundLink('https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea/wiki/Basic-Setup-and-Running'); return false;">build the project yourself</a> to generate your own patients. For help or more information, <a href="mailto:synthea-list@groups.mitre.org">contact us</a>!
