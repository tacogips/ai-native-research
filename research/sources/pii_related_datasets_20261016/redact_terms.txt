# REDACT Dataset Terms

Version 1.0 - August 2026

These terms apply to the REDACT dataset artifacts, annotations, corpus files, and the REDACT pattern catalog distributed through this repository or official mirrors, including Hugging Face.

The software used to generate, validate, or evaluate the dataset is separately licensed under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

## 1. Purpose

REDACT is released to support research, evaluation, auditing, education, and defensive privacy work involving personal-information detection.

The corpus is synthetic and is not intended to contain real personal data. Some identifiers and surface forms are deliberately structurally realistic, including checksum-valid or locale-specific formats, because realistic structure is necessary for detector evaluation.

## 2. Permitted uses

Subject to these terms, you may use, copy, analyze, transform, and create derivatives of the dataset for purposes including:

- scientific research
- benchmarking and evaluation
- privacy and compliance auditing
- education
- development and testing of defensive privacy, redaction, and PII-detection systems
- reproducibility studies and responsible extensions of the benchmark

## 3. Prohibited uses

You may not use the dataset or pattern catalog to:

- impersonate, defraud, harass, or otherwise harm a real person or organization
- generate, validate, or operationalize real credentials, identity documents, payment credentials, account credentials, or identifiers for fraudulent or abusive use
- evade, bypass, defeat, or materially assist the evasion of privacy, redaction, PII-detection, authentication, fraud-prevention, or security controls
- re-identify, deanonymize, or link benchmark content to a real person
- construct or enrich dossiers about real individuals from the synthetic examples
- facilitate unlawful discrimination, surveillance, profiling, or other unlawful processing of personal data
- use the resource in violation of applicable law or regulation

## 4. Redistribution and derivatives

You may redistribute the dataset or derivatives only if you:

1. preserve these REDACT Dataset Terms or provide a clear link to them;
2. identify the resource as REDACT or a derivative of REDACT;
3. do not represent synthetic identifiers as belonging to real individuals; and
4. do not remove notices that describe the resource as synthetic benchmark data.

## 6. Sensitivity labels

`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, and `LOW` are operational benchmark labels for evaluation slices. They are not legal advice, model-confidence scores, or universal rankings of re-identification risk.
